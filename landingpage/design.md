# Design — Peyek Artis

## Visual thesis
Modern artisan snack dossier: golden peyek, warm kitchen smoke, dark roasted-brown contrast, turmeric/chili accents, woven-bamboo/paper label texture, with soft Three.js crumbs/peanuts floating like the coffee bean particles in the reference.

## Domain artifact
- Nampan tiris after frying
- Rak toples / display warung
- Batch ticket / nota pesanan
- Label kemasan pouch/toples
- Packing checklist for shipping

## Composition model
Adapts the coffee project’s cinematic origin dossier into a peyek production dossier. The page moves from batch freshness to kitchen process to product lots to reseller readiness.

## First viewport
Full-canvas hero with peyek product photo, floating crumbs/peanuts, batch ticket card, and headline focused on “peyek renyah siap jual”.

## Section grammar
- Batch board: fresh batch, varian, kemasan, buyer type
- Dapur story: homemade, resep, bahan, consistency
- Frying station flow: adonan, tabur topping, goreng tipis, tiris, sortir, packing
- Product lots: Kacang, Rebon/Teri, Pedas/Custom
- Packing ledger: anti remuk, pouch/toples, label, reseller
- WhatsApp inquiry / reseller request

## Motion thesis
- Hero staged reveal + Three.js crumb field.
- GSAP reveal and parallax on product images.
- Horizontal flow feeling for frying steps on desktop, stacked on mobile.

## Anti-template gate
Passed. Avoiding generic food landing page and hero + product cards. Dominant grammar is batch kitchen dossier + reseller order board, adapted from coffee origin-processing model but with peyek-specific artifacts.
